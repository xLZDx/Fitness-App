1.3 What the product does today

  • Body scan: the body-fat estimate is provisional (Deurenberg formula: height, weight, age, sex), a ±5 point range, computed on the device, photos do not enter it. The cloud path is off behind a flag.
  • Nutrition plan: a calorie range, macro ranges, a protein minimum, a 6,000-12,000 steps goal, a weekly review with a stated reason and a 7-day undo. Excluded groups get a neutral talk-to-a-professional message without numbers.
  • A meal-slot food diary: search, quick log, saved foods; meal photo, barcode, label and describe-by-text via server functions that are off behind flags.
  • Privacy: face blur that fails closed, EXIF and GPS stripped from uploads, deletion on request, paged export, retention purge, account-deletion sweep.
  • Multisport foundation (no screens): activity model, sport types, sources and dedup merge. The nutrition hook for training programmes is wired into programme_builder with a neutral default.

1.5 Defects found by tests and fixed

  • Firestore rules: writing food_entries was impossible because of the expression limit.
  • Rules: withdrawing cloudConsent was refused although the design allows it.
  • EXIF survived in blurred and prepared JPEGs (two places).
  • Cancelling a local-only scan emitted a server delete without a document; Delete on the failed scan card did nothing.
  • Cyrillic serving units were not parsed; eggplant was tagged as an egg allergen; malt liquor was not tagged as alcohol.
  • The diary day follower never fired after midnight (a lazy field); the confidence row in the result card overflowed at 2x text scale and in Russian.
  • Also: compile errors in blind-written code (fixed), the ICU # in ARB, doubled gen:gen: food references, wrong vectors in the coverage map.
