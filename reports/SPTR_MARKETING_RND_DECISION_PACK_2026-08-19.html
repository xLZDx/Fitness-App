How to use this pack

Three rules govern everything below.

  1. Every public sentence carries a class. Nothing reaches a real user without one. The four classes are defined in §02 and applied in §03.
  2. An unbuilt idea is not disqualified. The purpose of this phase was to find what tomorrow's SPTR should be able to promise and then build it before advertising starts. A concept dies from infeasibility, irrationality, regulatory impossibility or inconsistency with the product — never from not existing yet.
  3. Claims are sorted by verification route, not by how many people said them. Source-verified, inspectable, lexical, hypothesis. A count of respondents — synthetic or otherwise — is never itself a reason to believe something.

What this pack deliberately does not contain: a chosen headline, any conversion or demand estimate, any ranking of the three concepts, and any claim about what proportion of users will interpret anything a particular way. Those require real respondents, and no work done so far can substitute.

01

The system

The durable conclusion of eleven rounds is that SPTR does not win on one atomic feature. It wins by owning a relationship with a specific piece of equipment: understand it now, remember it later, show the basis, and turn what it cannot recognise into future coverage.

Not the app decided seat 4 but SPTR remembered the setup you saved. Unexplained personal data reads as surveillance — a real misread found in testing, on the one dimension where this product is genuinely strong.

04

Product truth: what recognition actually is

Two opposite simplifications were both wrong and both circulated for several rounds. The correct model has four paths with different ceilings, and one shared terminus.

printed-text anchor  →  needs legible words     →  ~28 of 69 registry types plausibly carry them
cloud (Gemini)       →  sees any machine, but    →  may answer only with ~75 canonical names or "unknown"
on-device fallback   →  10 classes
                                     ↓
              known catalogue category  →  result
              outside / unresolved      →  UNKNOWN  →  machine card  →  contribution loop
Why this is a better story than "our AI recognises everything"

The boundary is not a defect to hide — it is the mechanism by which coverage grows. An unresolvable answer degrades to unknown and opens a machine card rather than becoming a guess. That behaviour is shipped, it is the honest half of the trust doctrine, and it is the only route by which the catalogue expands. The moat hypothesis rests on it.

Caveat that must stay attached: the cloud path has never been measured in production. The two recognition figures that have circulated measure the on-device model and an unshipped 29-class variant respectively. No accuracy number may be published until the live pipeline is measured — see §08.

05

Two features that must never be blurred again

Prior rounds repeatedly merged these under "setup guidance". They are different products, different data, different promises, and different risk.

SaysHow this machine works — where the seat adjusts, what the pad controlsHow you set this machine up last time

NeedsReliable model scope; category guidance may be too coarse where models differMRD-03 physical-unit identity + MRD-04 flexible values
Marketing roleEducation — supports acquisitionConvenience — supports retention
RiskDrifts toward health advice if written as "correct" or "safe"Reads as surveillance if provenance is not visible
Do not design around a numbered seat

Real equipment carries numbered detents, lettered positions, unmarked holes, continuous sliders, gas levers, worn labels, or nothing at all — welded and unbranded machines are common in exactly the gyms this product is most useful in. A schema of seatPosition: int encodes an assumption the physical world does not honour.

Consequence for copy as well as schema: say "remembers your setup", never "Seat 4 on every machine".

06

The memory ladder — and the rung that is nearly free

"It remembers this machine" is three different products. Naming the rungs separately is what makes the roadmap tractable.

Level 1 · equipment-type memory
"Your last leg press: 45 kg × 10."

This needs no schema change. WorkoutLogEntry already carries exerciseId, and 1,384 of 1,887 exercises already carry equipmentId. Type-level memory is a join across data that exists today — not a migration, not a new entity. It was assumed to be a build; it is a query.

constraint A different leg press has different leverage, stack calibration and geometry. Never imply physical equivalence at this level.

Level 2 · gym-machine memory
"At this gym's leg press, you used 45 kg."

Needs gym identity plus a workout→equipment relationship. A gymId string already exists in buddy_match.dart and equipment_report.dart — but no code path ever assigns it a real value; it defaults to 'unknown'. The field name exists; the identity does not. Treat this as greenfield with a name to reuse.

Level 3 · physical-unit setup memory
"Seat 4 · pad 2 · 45 kg — the setup you saved here."

Needs a physical machine instance, flexible setup fields, a way to create and confirm them, and an answer for machines with no markings. Higher cost than any prior round implied: this is a machine-instance data problem, not a content problem.

Recognition history compounds the gap: it is keyed by equipmentId — one row per catalogue type — so two lat pulldowns in one gym, and the same type across two gyms, collapse to a single identity today.

07

The brand, and its documented risk

SPTR = Spotter is kept. It is the operator's hypothesis and nothing found so far justifies abandoning it.

Established without any panel: in gym English, spotter can mean the person who physically assists a lift. The brand therefore carries an available physical-spotting reading. That is a lexical fact.

Not established, and not claimable: how many users make that reading, whether it helps or harms trust, whether it creates safety expectations, whether Russian-speaking users process the English word the same way. A synthetic panel converged on it, and that convergence was then traced to three properties of the instrument — a probe that described spotting in plain words, a text medium that over-weights a wordmark against an absent image, and a probe edit that alone explained the reversal from an earlier round. It is a controlled brand hypothesis, not a measured harm.

Standing constraints on brand expression

Teach the meaning through what the product does — helps you read unfamiliar equipment, brings back useful context, shows the basis for uncertain answers. Never through imagery or language suggesting a lone lifter under a heavy bar, physical rescue, supervision of a dangerous lift, or replacement of a human spotter. The name is allowed to promise attentiveness; it is never allowed to promise catching the bar.

Model sampling of the abbreviation — asking repeatedly what SPTR resolves to — is retained only as ambiguity enumeration: it surfaces plausible expansions worth designing against. It is not decodability evidence. Frequency across samples measures a language model's association distribution, not a population. Written plainly because it is exactly the kind of number that gets quoted as a percentage two weeks later.

11

Pre-public truth gate

Runs once, immediately before the first real advertising. Nothing aspirational reaches real users until the corresponding build and proof exist.

  1. Every sentence on the page has a class from §02. Anything unclassified is not shipped.
  2. No Committed claim appears publicly unless it is built and verified at source on the release commit. Prototype visuals showing unbuilt behaviour never leave internal review.
  3. No Hypothesis language appears publicly in any form.
  4. Every numeric claim names what was measured, on what, and when. No figure that describes an unshipped asset or an unmeasured path.
  5. Health-claim sweep: no page text implies injury prevention, physical supervision, medical suitability, or correctness for a body. Mechanical description only.
  6. Both locales pass the same gate. Russian is not a translation of an approved page — it is a page that must be approved. Register carries the risk in both A and B.
  7. Disclosure check: cloud upload, sandbox payments, and platform scope stated where a reasonable user would look.

13

What the process actually produced

The visible output was eleven documents. The useful output was four things, and only one of them is a marketing artifact.

  • A verified product-truth baseline. Which recognition figures describe what; which claims survive contact with the source; where the shipped behaviour is stronger than the documentation said, and where weaker.
  • A backlog that marketing generated. MRD-01 through MRD-07 exist because a positioning idea was taken seriously enough to check whether it could ever be true. That is the mechanism the R&D mode was created to produce, and it worked.
  • A method that survives its own findings. Sorting by verification route rather than by agreement; deleting the instruments that manufacture false confidence rather than disclaiming them; demoting a headline within the hour when its own design predicted it.
  • Three concepts worth real money to test — and the discipline not to pick one on evidence that cannot bear it.
The formula, frozen

SPTR — short for Spotter. It helps you understand the equipment in front of you, remembers the context that becomes useful when you come back, shows what its answer is based on, and turns what it cannot recognise yet into what it will cover next.

It earns the name by being useful, contextual and honest — never by implying it replaces the person who physically spots a lift.
