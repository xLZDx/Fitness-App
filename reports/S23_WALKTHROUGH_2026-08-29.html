False alarms ruled out before reporting

"Form Coach is a sustainer benefit" is not a mistranslation

Checked against app_ru.arb: "sustainer" (Russian "покровитель") is this app's established term for its paid support tier, used consistently across 7+ other strings ("Become a sustainer", "Sustainer feed", etc.). Deliberate product terminology, not a localization bug.

No live camera feed or skeleton drawn in coach mode — not a defect

Confirmed, documented behavior of _AvatarBackdrop/_PoseAvatar (form_check_page.dart:849-966, dated 2026-08-15): the camera is never shown directly by design (privacy), and the figure only draws once MediaPipe places a body in frame. During this check the phone was not physically pointed at a person, so no figure drew — expected, not broken.

Progress tab empty state is correct

After the account reset (pm clear) the screen honestly shows "0" everywhere with placeholder copy ("Log a workout to see the trend") — expected behavior for a fresh account, not a bug.
