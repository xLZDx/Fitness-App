1 · D-05B reproduced, and it points at something bigger than one chip

The Library tab's "Тренер по технике" (Technique Coach) filter chip was reproduced live on the S8: a clean first-tap PASS, and a clean first-tap FAIL after a fresh app relaunch — the exact historical symptom ("chip stayed on 'Для вас'," a second tap works). A side investigation into why a batch of automated trials went unresponsive (ruled out adb itself — KEYCODE_HOME fired correctly mid-freeze) surfaced a third, unrelated widget with the same shape of failure: the bottom-navigation "ТРЕНИРОВКИ" tab failed to register on its very first tap immediately after cold start, 3 times out of 3, each from a completely fresh app process.

Three different widgets (login button, a filter chip, a bottom-nav tab), same symptom: the first tap right after a screen becomes visible sometimes does nothing; an identical second tap works. D-05B is not scored to a rate the way D-03 was — n=2 clean trials plus 3 adjacent bottom-nav failures is real evidence, not a percentage.

2 · Form Coach: verified, not just surveyed

An earlier, lighter pass had called the pose-detection feature "complete and wired" without deep verification. A dedicated skeptical review confirmed it file:line, end to end:

  • Real ML Kit: google_mlkit_pose_detection is a real dependency; the service instantiates an actual PoseDetector, streams real camera frames, converts real landmarks.
  • Real logic: pose_gate.dart's geometric checks and rep_counter.dart's hysteresis state machine are genuine, not placeholders.
  • Real navigation: routed, reachable from real screens, production-wired at bootstrap (the mock provider is only the test default).
  • Real voice coaching: a real flutter_tts integration, not aspirational.
  • 35 substantive test files, no unresolved TODO/stub markers in production source.
One caveat carried from the code's own comments, not invented here: gate thresholds are documented as "opening values … not measured on a device fleet" — real and working, not clinically tuned.

4 · M7's accessibility half: scoped, and better than expected

This codebase already has a dedicated accessibility test suite, WCAG-ratio-cited color tokens, a required-tooltip icon-button component, and a model implementation for its custom slider widgets. The scoping pass found one real gap worth prioritizing:

  1. MajorReduced motion is never respected. Zero references to disableAnimations/reduceMotion anywhere in the app. Several custom HUD animations (background, toggles, onboarding entrances) always animate regardless of the OS accessibility setting — a real issue for vestibular-disorder users. Consistent coverage across every animated surface is its own scoped gate, not a one-line patch.
  2. MinorA bare, unlabeled gesture area on the onboarding body-zone diagram — likely intentional (a fully-labeled equivalent chip grid sits right beside it, and the widget's own comment says so), flagged for a product-intent confirmation rather than assumed broken.
  3. MinorA few compact icon buttons may render under the app's own 44px minimum-tap-target floor — needs an actual measurement pass before it's a confirmed fix.

Contrast, color-only signaling, icon-button labeling, and custom slider semantics were all checked and confirmed already covered, with evidence — not silently assumed clean.
