dd12695 — internal SPTR site prototype, three concept cells

A static shell added to the existing Firebase Hosting root (public/), carrying three equal-quality landing-page concepts switched by ?concept=a|b|c, with ?lang=en|ru and ?debug=1 as internal controls. No hero was picked — ranking the cells needs real respondents, so this ships as an R&D instrument, not a launch asset. Additions only; the seven pre-existing files under public/ were untouched.

Every classified sentence carries a claim-state attribute in source (12 exists, 4 committed, 1 hypothesis at the time of this commit). All four product images are crops from the operator's own gym photographs — no fabricated evidence.

Verified in Chrome via Playwright across 3 concepts × 2 languages × 2 viewports: one visible hero per cell, zero horizontal overflow, zero external requests, zero console errors, reduced-motion and keyboard paths intact, 67/67 translation keys present, 142 KB initial transfer identical across all three cells.

9db5902 — split claim class into truth vs. public-eligibility, add fail-closed lint

Post-implementation review of the prototype found two governance defects: a single classification axis conflated "is this true" with "may this exact wording be shown publicly," and an unclassified claim could silently vanish from the hand-regenerated register instead of failing a check.

Replaced with two independent axes on every classified sentence — data-claim-state (exists / committed / hypothesis / never) and data-public-gate (allowed / qualify / blocked / internal-only) — with core/marketing/claim_register.json as the single source of truth and scripts/marketing/claim_lint.py failing closed on drift. All 22 claims were re-adjudicated individually rather than bulk-migrated, which caught a real overclaim: "most machines have their own name printed on them" was not supported by the ~28-of-69 field measurement behind it, and was corrected to "many… when SPTR can" in both languages.

02 Gate D — equipment-type workout memory

shipped

2cc271b · MRD-01/02 (type-level slice)

Shows a recognized equipment type's most recent logged workout on the equipment detail page — "Your last leg press: 45 kg × 10" — never a specific gym or physical machine; that stays deferred to MRD-02/03/04. Matches client-side against the already-watched windowed session history and the existing exercise→equipmentId catalog mapping: zero new Firestore reads, zero schema change. A miss inside a genuinely complete window reads as a confirmed "never done this equipment"; a miss inside a truncated window reads as "not found in your recent workouts" — the two are never conflated.

Independent review (flutter-reviewer, type-design-analyzer, silent-failure-hunter — parallel, no cross-seeding)

  • MAJOR

    The truncation check compared exercise-row count to session count. Multi-exercise sessions (since R11e) could make a truncated history window silently read as complete.

    Fixed: distinct session-id count, not raw row count. Mutation-tested.
  • MAJOR

    The exercise-id match set only read the vendored catalog, permanently missing the 11 of 48 equipment types that fall through to AI-generated exercises.

    Fixed: unions the cached AI-generated exercise ids. Mutation-tested.
  • MINOR

    A load-order race could flash a false "not found" before the session stream's first snapshot arrived.

    Fixed: gates on the stream's loading state. Mutation-tested.

Full findings, evidence, and fix-by-fix mutation proof: core/product/GATE_D_EQUIPMENT_TYPE_HISTORY_D0_NOTE_2026-08-19.md.

Marketing claim memory-last-lift reclassified committed/internal-only → exists/qualify to match; claim_lint still 22/22 clean. flutter analyze clean; full suite 2445/2445 (2439 + 6 new regression tests, each proven via revert-and-confirm-fails mutation testing).

03 Gate E — shared uncertainty contract

shipped

105f9d4 · MRD-06

Answer<T, R> (mobile/lib/shared/uncertainty/answer.dart) — a sealed, exhaustiveness-checked type for "a confident value, or an explicit domain-owned closed-set reason why not." This is the shape three independent existing systems (ScanOutcome, PoseGateVerdict, VideoFailureReason) had already reinvented without ever referencing each other — that convergence, not any one system, is what justified formalizing a shared layer.

Deliberately not a numeric confidence score: the codebase already carries two non-comparable 0–1 "confidence" scales inside a single feature, one explicitly documented as "not a probability" — direct evidence against a universal numeric model.

Ships the contract type only. Does not retrofit the five existing mature uncertainty systems (visual equipment, form check, safety screening, set capture, video failure) in this gate — each is individually calibrated and safety- or trust-adjacent, and none of the null-returning cases currently carries an explicit reason, so adopting the contract there would mean inventing new reason taxonomies as an unrequested side effect. Zero existing production files touched.

Independent review (type-design-analyzer)

  • MAJOR

    Unbounded T/R let T be instantiated as nullable, reopening the exact ambiguity the type exists to close — Answer<String?, R>.confident(null) became indistinguishable from an uncertain answer through valueOrNull.

    Fixed: Answer<T extends Object, R extends Object>. Verified via a temporary compile-fail probe, then the fix is enforced by the compiler itself.
  • MINOR

    Phantom-R equality/hashCode asymmetry on the confident arm.

    Deferred per the reviewer's own recommendation — zero call sites today.

Full reconnaissance and reasoning: core/product/GATE_E_SHARED_UNCERTAINTY_D0_NOTE_2026-08-19.md.

flutter analyze clean; full suite 2459/2459 (2445 + 14 new); two mutation proofs (valueOrNull leaking bestGuess, isConfident hardcoded true), both caught by the suite, confirmed by reverting each and re-running.
