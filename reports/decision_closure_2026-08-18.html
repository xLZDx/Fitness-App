Why the obvious fix did not work

_member_body slices from a signature to its matching brace, which is right for a Dart method and wrong for a Cloud Function: onCall( takes an options object first, so the slice returns 317 characters of secrets: configuration and no handler at all. The replacement, _exported_member, slices to the next top-level export — measured as lines 562–758, containing createCheckoutSession and nothing of startFreeTrial beyond one comment that _without_comments strips anyway.
