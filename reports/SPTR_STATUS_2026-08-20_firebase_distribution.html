04 What was NOT done this pass, and why

The 54-section directive additionally asks for: a full Onboarding v4 implementation (9 screens × 2 themes) with a new mandatory architecture decision on where the PAR-Q+ safety gate sits, existing-user data migration, mutation tests, an independent review panel, branch archaeology and salvage across two foreign branches in two repositories, then deleting those branches and worktrees.

None of that was touched this pass:

  • Branch deletion is pre-authorized, but the directive's own §36 requires a ledger proving no unique work is lost first — that ledger doesn't exist yet; it is real classification work, not a formality.
  • Onboarding v4 is a genuine multi-gate feature with a safety-architecture decision (the directive itself labels it PRODUCT_REQUIRED_EXTENSION_TO_HANDOFF) and existing-user field migration — to be implemented gate by gate, not in one unverified pass.
