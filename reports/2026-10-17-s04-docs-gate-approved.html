What the reviewer found

Main theme: what happens to a recording when Keep and Delete are pressed together, and on a crash in the middle of cleanup. Outcome: the decision is fixed once by a file created with strict first-writer-wins semantics; cleanup is one atomic directory rename; one process-lifetime lock protects the journal from a second instance and a separate short lock coordinates decision publication.

Root cause of most rounds: each fix checked only the named defect, not every lifecycle path. The lesson is recorded in core/LESSONS_LOG.md.
