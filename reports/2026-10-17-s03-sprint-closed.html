What the external reviewer found

1. While a workout of another account was running, the side rail and the Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R shortcut still opened Record. Fixed; the regression test found a 16 dp menu shift in the first fix, fixed in a separate commit.

2. Two quick mode writes of one account could finish out of order and leave the older value stored. Writes now run in order.
