What happened here

RECOG-C1 ended on a negative result: across 104 observations the recogniser answered unknown not once, and neither its stated confidence, nor the lead over the second candidate, nor the number of alternatives separated frames that have a single right answer from frames that do not. The abstention signal cannot come from the model's opinion of itself.

SO1 asks whether a second, independent model supplies it: do two models disagree more often on frames where no single machine is the subject than on frames where one is? It is a pre-registered hypothesis test, not the beginning of an ensemble implementation — GPT-PM's framing, adopted verbatim. Nothing in production changes.

Why this decided the outcome rather than merely refining the picture

GPT-PM set a power floor: at least 20 unique source photographs in each ground-truth kind of each arm, or the result is INCONCLUSIVE whatever the numbers say. The whole corpus gives 26 and 26. The people-free subset would have given 16 and 15 — pre-registered to return INCONCLUSIVE no matter what Qwen produced. The operator's choice was not "more statistics" as a preference; it was the only option under which SO1 can reach a verdict at all.

The survey's own limit

One visual pass by one reviewer over downscaled contact-sheet tiles, not the full-resolution originals. real_people = no means "no person visible at tile resolution", not a guarantee of absence. A far better lower bound than the label regex, and still a lower bound.

GPT-PM's five findings, and how each was closed

The first plan came back VERDICT: REVISE — 1 BLOCKER, 4 MAJOR. All five accepted without argument.

BLOCKER — the gate checked that the operator answered, not that they authorised

A well-formed record saying do_not_send would have satisfied a presence-and-shape check. decision is now a closed enum whole_corpus | people_free_only | deny, and deny, an unknown value, a missing field, an unparseable file, an absent file, an undefined property, or a binding to the wrong manifest or pre-registration each refuse — evaluated before any image byte is read and before the HTTP client is constructed. Shown, not asserted: on a deny record the transport is never constructed and no output file appears.

MAJOR 1 — recording a hash after sending is forensics, not a frozen-dataset rule

A 104-row observation manifest carries the expected sha256 of every image, derived from the frozen RECOG-C1 arm manifest. The runner rehashes the bytes and re-verifies the prompt, vocabulary and configuration seal immediately before each request. A one-byte change refuses that observation and sends nothing.

MAJOR 2 — the power floor was promised and not implemented

It is the one rule that fires exactly when a consent choice shrinks the data. It is now executable and can override a numerically passing result. It also exposed that the people_free_only policy had no input at all: no frozen artefact carried a people flag. Hence the survey above.

MAJOR 3 — the vocabulary generator was in the verification and absent from scope

The vocabulary is now derived from production CANONICAL_MACHINES and the alias registry by a port of EquipmentAliasIndex.normalise and resolve (passes 1–2), and the suite cross-checks that port against the shipped TypeScript one by running it, over 78 probes. 71 canonical names resolve to 69 ids: plyo box/aerobic step and parallettes/push-up blocks collapse, so scoring raw strings would have manufactured two disagreement classes that are agreements.

MAJOR 4 — "the runner never opens the ground-truth file" proves one thing about one file

The runner's input schema is now closed — exactly five fields, any extra column refuses — and a capturing transport inspects the serialized request body: the frozen prompt, the frozen machine list, exactly one image, and no occurrence of a ground-truth kind, a first-model answer, an arm hint or a scorer field.

The decision regions, fixed in advance

PASS
all five required:
  • D_multiple ≥ 60%
  • D_canonical ≤ 20%
  • Δ ≥ 40 pp
  • the direction holds separately in both arms
  • source-photo-clustered permutation test, p < 0.05
FAIL
any one suffices:
  • D_multiple < 50%
  • D_canonical > 25%
  • Δ < 25 pp
  • Qwen accuracy < 80%, or more than 10 pp worse than Gemini
  • the direction reverses in either arm
INCONCLUSIVE
everything else — a real outcome, and not permission to tune thresholds on these same 52 photographs. A threshold chosen after the data is seen is not a threshold. Separately: fewer than 20 unique sources in either kind of either arm returns INCONCLUSIVE however good the estimates look.

The evidence that the guards are load-bearing

scripts/dev/recog_so1.tests.py — 51 checks, no network call anywhere. Three guards are shown failing under mutation and passing on the real code:

  • removing the power floor turns the underpowered fixture into a PASS;
  • weakening the clustering to per-observation collapses the permutation p;
  • counting an unresolved observation as disagreement turns a run that mostly did not work into a significant result in the hypothesised direction.

The file closes with test_the_test: a verdict() stubbed to always return PASS, and a consent gate stubbed to always authorise, must each be rejected by every expectation written against them. A check that cannot fail proves nothing.

What remains, and what blocks it

The run is not authorised

GPT-PM's GO covers this gate explicitly and the real run explicitly not. For 104 requests to leave, core/plans/RECOG_SO1_CONSENT.json must exist and satisfy the state machine: an affirmative decision, the operator's verbatim words, a binding to the manifest digest 367af387…, a binding to the pre-registration digest, and the recorded Data Controls state. Both substantive answers already exist; what remains is writing them into that record and confirming it.

After that, only the corpus pass (roughly 26–40 minutes against the measured ceiling of 8000 tokens per minute), and the scorer printing a verdict by the bars above and by nothing else. The configuration is now frozen outright: if the provider stops accepting it, SO1 stops and requires a new pre-registration revision — a 4xx is an experiment failure, not permission to edit the file mid-run.
