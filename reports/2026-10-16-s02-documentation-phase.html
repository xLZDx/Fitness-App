What the internal reviews found

RoundResultMain findings in plain words
1 (architect, reliability, security)24 findings, all acceptedA deleted activity could come back from another device. An upload finishing after account erasure brought GPS data back. Expiry could outrun the user's setting. Heart rate was guarded by a strip list instead of a closed schema. The only device copy was lost on sign-out.
2 (closing check)21 findings, all applied in revision 3My revision counter could wipe live data because a permission error was read as a deletion. The delete fence did not match the real account deletion flow. Two parallel commits could overwrite each other. Expiry recompute could not find users. Firestore rules limit for batched writes.

Decisions taken: a server tombstone (TTL 400 days), the delete fence in a separate server-only collection, a permission error never wipes data without a tombstone, write batches of at most 10, a seventh hardened callable for bulk stream deletion.
