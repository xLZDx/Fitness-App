GPT-PM's decision

Option (d): phased reconciliation, a hard boundary

  • Phase 1 — authorized in this gate

    Goal, Level/Experience, Schedule, Personal height/weight, Equipment (presentation-only — same values, same provider writes, same eligibility inputs).

  • body, healthFlags, screening, preview — frozen structurally AND visually

    No reordering, grouping, medically-meaningful label changes, required/optional status, skip logic, default values, navigation conditions, or disclosure-boundary changes. Screening/PAR-Q+ named explicitly as a surface that must not even be "nicely merged" with a neighbouring screen — its own redesign, if any, is a separate future gate with a regression contract on all 7 answers and refusal routing. Preview/refusal likewise deferred: the reference's "Ready" screen may supply visual language only for the happy path, not the state model — production must keep ready and blocked/refused as distinct states.

  • Dead data stays untouched

    A field going unread by the eligibility engine today is not grounds to delete or merge it — that's a separate product decision, especially for free-text medical fields.

  • 9 screens is explicitly NOT the goal

    This gate may end at 11 surfaces and that's fine. Success metric: 5 direct mappings visually reconciled, zero safety/data semantic change.
