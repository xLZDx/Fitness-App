Side finding: 4 alerts could never have fired

Mechanism. firebase-functions/logger's logger.error(EVENT, {…}) unconditionally rewrites jsonPayload.message into "Error: EVENT\n<stack>" for ERROR severity. An alert filter built as an exact match (messageEquals) against the raw event string therefore never matched the real log line.

Same root cause already found and fixed for enforcement_state at Step 10A — but 4 other already-deployed alerts (Stripe reconciliation, delete-account, export-account, canary probe) were left untouched until this session.

Item #9: deliberately not deployed — and why that's correct

All 4 AI Gateway functions (aiCoachAdvice, aiEquipmentRecognition, aiMachineDescription, aiExerciseGeneration) are written, tested, and exported — but not deployed to production. A plan to deploy now and close the item "for real" was sent to GPT-PM for review. GPT-PM held that plan: the model in source (gemini-3-flash-preview) is already documented as deprecated, the AI-specific App Check flag isn't enforced yet, and per-user quota alone doesn't survive anonymous-account rotation. Deploying 4 new paid endpoints just to turn a table row green would have substituted an observability gate's own judgment for an actual production-release decision.

Outcome: the direct-client-Gemini-call half is genuinely closed (G1's migration confirmed — zero direct Gemini calls anywhere in the mobile code). The production-deployment half is deferred to a new mandatory gate.
