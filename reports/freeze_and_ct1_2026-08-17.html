Six reopened. Every fix carries a test that was run before the fix and watched to fail; that is the bar, and it was met in every case.

Two patterns, not seven incidents

A field reaches the model and the serialiser and not the guard. N-01 and N-02 are the same defect in two languages. F014's field was added to toJson/copyWith and not fromJson; then to fromJson and not operator ==; and the health map grew two fields the Firestore rule never learned about. Nothing in Dart or in the rules language checks "did you list the field again", and every instance passed the analyzer, the suite and review.

A guard matching a substring of prose is evidence about the substring. Deleting the entire Firestore rules CI job left its test green — the word "rules" appears five times in comments. Deleting the whole-suite step left its test green — flutter test also matches the integration job, which runs one file and is skipped on push. contains('SHIPPED') was satisfied by NOT SHIPPED.

Neither pattern was found by reading. Both were found by breaking the thing and watching the test stay green. Both now have structural defences: a source-scanning field-coverage test that fails when a declared field is missing from any serialiser or from equality, and five guards rewritten to match commands rather than words.

Checked and sound

Negative results are part of the evidence.

  • F016 / AI trust boundary. Every MachineCard consumer enumerated — all render-only or card-store-only. No model output can mint, mutate or prescribe a canonical exercise identity.
  • Model governance. The bundled artefact's sha256 and byte count recomputed and matched exactly; committed blob matches the worktree; the load path is the registry's path; no code loads v2 and no document claims it ships; training_code_commit: UNKNOWN intact.
  • Entitlements and receipts. Server-only, double-closed, denial-tested per verb. No store-receipt path exists; entitlement flows only from a signature-verified webhook whose uid is stamped server-side. No replay, no cross-account binding.
  • F014 wording. Neither locale carries any forbidden term. Reasons are a typed enum through an exhaustive switch, so an unhandled case fails to compile.

The champion is a rule set by decision

Every check is exact, so its precision against its own definition is 1.0 and reporting that number would be meaningless. Training a classifier to detect a missing key would be a slower, less precise, unexplainable reimplementation of 'contraindications' not in row. EVALUATION_LABEL_GAP

Precision, recall and false-positive rate are not reported, and the absence is the finding. With zero reviewed labels, any figure would be computed against the baseline's own output — the self-referential loop the label contract forbids. Computing it while forbidding it would be a strange sort of principle.

Roughly 150–200 reviewed rows from the holdout split would close it. Nothing else is blocking, and no further code substitutes for a person.
