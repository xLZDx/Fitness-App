Section 0 — critical flags

Read this first

Ten facts that change how to read everything else in this document.

The release-readiness verdict was never lifted

The full project audit from 08-16 returned a NOT_RELEASE_READY verdict, confirmed by an independent review of that same audit. The 5-gate remediation plan (G-A…G-E, section 6) was "Not started, awaiting REMEDIATION-GO" as of 08-16. That is now known to be wrong for all five (see the green flags below): implementation evidence exists for every gate, just filed under finding IDs rather than the audit's gate names — the 7 research agents' "not started" read came from checking for the gate names literally, not for the underlying work. Degree varies a lot, though, and is not "all basically done": G-D is formally closed (a genuine GPT-PM verdict on it AS A NAMED GATE); G-C/F016's reviewed hazard is closed — a real code fix shipped and GPT-PM issued a genuine `VERDICT: APPROVE`, but narrower wording than G-D's ("reviewed hazard: CLOSED," not "G-C: FORMALLY CLOSED as a gate") — no standalone named-gate closure verdict exists for G-C yet, same governance gap as G-A/G-B/G-E; G-A, G-B and G-E have substantial implementation evidence but no formal named-gate verdict yet, with G-A additionally carrying one genuinely open item (exact chest-pain copy text). Detail on each below.

Regulatory Gate J: DO NOT SHIP

A separate regulatory audit (08-15) returned "REGULATORY_REVIEW_REQUIRED — DO NOT SHIP." Undisclosed Health Connect access (steps/HR/sleep) in the privacy policy in any locale; the About page's "rehab-grade exercise guidance" claim directly contradicts the app's own Terms (medical-device reclassification risk, MDR Rule 11). No document found confirms a fix.

Two gate-numbering systems share the same names — do not confuse them

Track A (actually executed, closed): "G1–G4" / "M1–M9" is the MVP delivery track (AI Gateway, Android 16, HUD migration, IAM). Track B (formal architecture, core/agent-engine-v2.3.1/ + .claude/policies/fitness-recommendation-gates.json): "G0–G11" is the Safety/Eligibility/Prescription recommendation engine, with 29 bound specialist agents and veto authority held by the clinical gate. Track B has not started — no report describes closing any of its gates.

Two BLOCKERs with no confirmed closure in the decision log

P1.G5 (SPTR exact-equipment-identity catalog) — blocked since 08-22 on insufficient direct sources for Technogym/Panatta (manufacturer sites return HTTP 403); status never changes later in the log. 4 identical silent alerts (2× Stripe reconciliation, delete-account, export-account, canary) from MVP1.G3 Step 10A round 8 — found, filed to backlog, never shown fixed.

The "closed → actually wasn't" pattern repeats systematically

Not a one-off: bug D-03 (dropped tap) was closed and reopened at least 4 times in a row; Step 10A was declared closed and immediately self-corrected ("declaration above was premature"); N-01 (BLOCKER) turned out to be an earlier fix (F014) "lost on restart."
