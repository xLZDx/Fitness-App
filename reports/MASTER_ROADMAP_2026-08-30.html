Section 0 — critical flags

Read this first

Six facts that change how to read everything else in this document.

The release-readiness verdict was never lifted

The full project audit from 08-16 returned a NOT_RELEASE_READY verdict, confirmed by an independent review of that same audit. The 5-gate remediation plan (G-A…G-E, section 6) was "Not started, awaiting REMEDIATION-GO" as of 08-16 — none of the 7 research agents found any trace of that chain having been run since.

Regulatory Gate J: DO NOT SHIP

A separate regulatory audit (08-15) returned "REGULATORY_REVIEW_REQUIRED — DO NOT SHIP." Undisclosed Health Connect access (steps/HR/sleep) in the privacy policy in any locale; the About page's "rehab-grade exercise guidance" claim directly contradicts the app's own Terms (medical-device reclassification risk, MDR Rule 11). No document found confirms a fix.

Two gate-numbering systems share the same names — do not confuse them

Track A (actually executed, closed): "G1–G4" / "M1–M9" is the MVP delivery track (AI Gateway, Android 16, HUD migration, IAM). Track B (formal architecture, core/agent-engine-v2.3.1/ + .claude/policies/fitness-recommendation-gates.json): "G0–G11" is the Safety/Eligibility/Prescription recommendation engine, with 29 bound specialist agents and veto authority held by the clinical gate. Track B has not started — no report describes closing any of its gates.

Two BLOCKERs with no confirmed closure in the decision log

P1.G5 (SPTR exact-equipment-identity catalog) — blocked since 08-22 on insufficient direct sources for Technogym/Panatta (manufacturer sites return HTTP 403); status never changes later in the log. 4 identical silent alerts (2× Stripe reconciliation, delete-account, export-account, canary) from MVP1.G3 Step 10A round 8 — found, filed to backlog, never shown fixed.

The "closed → actually wasn't" pattern repeats systematically

Not a one-off: bug D-03 (dropped tap) was closed and reopened at least 4 times in a row; Step 10A was declared closed and immediately self-corrected ("declaration above was premature"); N-01 (BLOCKER) turned out to be an earlier fix (F014) "lost on restart." Practical takeaway: a "closed" status in an individual plan file is not proof by itself — proof is independent re-verification, which not every gate received twice.

Correction, 08-30 — G-A turned out to be mostly already done

The same pattern flagged above ("closed → actually wasn't") ran in reverse here: section 6 below describes G-A as "not started," quoting the 08-16 audit — but a direct code check on 08-30 (after this document was first published) found all three G-A items already implemented, just not filed under that label. ProgrammeNotViable is caught before the generic error handler and shows an EligibilityNotice instead of a Retry snackbar (comment "N01", workouts_page.dart:1300-1360); the real tagged/total safety-coverage figure renders (l10n.safetyFilterCoverage(coverage.tagged, coverage.total), safety_disclosure.dart:100-109, marked "F019" in-code); the SafetyDisclosure widget is wired into every exercise-serving surface via the shared exerciseReferenceSections function plus separate insertions in workout_player_page.dart/workouts_page.dart (marked "F020" in-code). This doesn't mean section 6 is wrong — the 08-16 audit was accurate when written — it means this remediation track shipped without an explicit G-A gate entry in DECISION_LOG, and section 6 below inherited the audit's stale status without re-checking current code. Full detail and method: core/DECISION_LOG.md, the "REMEDIATION: GPT-PM round-1 review" entry (08-30) and the G-A entry that follows it. G-D/G-C/G-B/G-E from the same audit have NOT been re-verified this way — their "not started" status stands as-is, from the audit.
