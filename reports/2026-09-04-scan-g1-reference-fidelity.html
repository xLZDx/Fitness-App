Regressions found and fixed along the way

MAJOR

The shadow/tracking fix touched the whole HUD, not just Scan

Fixing a real HudSurface defect (the glow bled through an almost-transparent panel fill) and a letter-spacing leak was applied globally — Home, Workouts, and ordinary HUD buttons/chips/nav-bar goldens all moved, even though R5 requires them to stay byte-identical.

Outcome: fixed — both fixes became opt-in via a new shadowOutsideOnly parameter and an explicit letterSpacing: 0 only at Scan's own call sites; all 14 non-Scan goldens were restored byte-identical, confirmed by a CI-matching container run.

MAJOR

The "read on the machine" honesty note rendered inside the canonical card

On a printed-text match, the match card grew below the boundary R6 actually measures — a production detail ended up inside the very surface the gate compares 1:1 against the reference.

Outcome: fixed — the note now renders below the primary button alongside other production elements; a new test asserts the card keeps its canonical shape and the note is never its descendant.

MAJOR

Methodology: the reference renderer substituted the app's font for the canonical one

While drafting a narrow threshold revision for aiming_dark, GPT-PM caught an earlier defect: the reference renderer had been substituting the app's static font cuts for the canonical variable Google Archivo — moving the reference toward the app instead of the other way around, which could mask a real app typography defect as "now they match."

Outcome: fixed — canonical Archivo restored; re-measuring after the revert changed nothing (within thousandths of a percent) — the substitution wasn't masking anything in practice, but the contract is now clean.

Approved exception: aiming_dark ≤ 3.2%

After every plausible fix attempt (fonts, letter-spacing, geometricPrecision, leading distribution), the residual difference (2.965% vs. the original ≤2.5%) stayed confined to text/glyph/hairline anti-aliasing edges across the whole frame — the signature of rasterizer noise between Chromium and Skia, not a geometry or token defect. Through a formal Rosetta revision (plan fitness_app-2026-09-04T16-17-19-994Z-588ce5), GPT-PM approved a narrow, single-frame exception — 3.2% instead of the shared threshold — with the other three frames and the mean-diff ceiling untouched.
