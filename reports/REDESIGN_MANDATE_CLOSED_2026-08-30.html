Why Form Coach isn't "unfinished"

The real blocker is a missing reliable judge, not a HUD defect

The coloured-verdict skeleton infrastructure was built, tested, and correctly gated on FormClassifier.canFault. But no exercise reachable through the Form Coach picker can produce a coloured verdict today: squat/deadlift by camera-angle-confound design, push-up by a separate, unrelated rep-counter defect. Investigation proved a prior claimed cause ("coordinate normalization") was wrong — both poses already share one isotropic contract. The real cause is viewpoint dependence: every authored target is a side view, while avatar mode's default stance is face-on. GPT-PM withdrew its own prior direction and routed the coloured verdict to a separate ML/R&D track — a deliberate architectural decision, not a forgotten task.

GPT-PM's decision

  • HUD/Figma REDESIGN MANDATE — CLOSED

    "I don't see a remaining item that, under our current governance decisions, still belongs to the redesign execution path."

  • "Redesign closed" != "product fully complete"

    Separate ML, safety, release, catalogue and product-roadmap gates continue independently and must not be held hostage to keep the redesign artificially open.

  • New governance rule

    "Do not reopen the HUD/Figma redesign mandate merely because the ML/R&D Form Coach verdict pipeline has not landed. Reopen only for a demonstrated visual regression, a new authoritative design handoff, or an explicit operator redesign request."
