Device evidence

Debug build of this tree, installed over the existing .debug package, driven by adb and uiautomator, screenshots via screencap. The playback criterion is the plan's own: the fraction of panel pixels whose RGB sum moved by more than 24/255 between two frames 1 s apart; above 2 % means moving.

A false alarm, kept on record

The session's first "live" screenshot showed the old white outline on the S23. The package in focus was verified before every screenshot after that.

Not obtained in-session

The panel's hand-over from the demonstration to the user's figure with a real body in frame. Nobody stood in front of either camera; the behaviour is proven by the widget tests in live_demo_test.dart (fade, then pause; back on losing the body). Per the plan's evidence semantics this does not gate.

  • A rejected pause() left the decoder running. FACT: _fail only hid the view and disabled _sync for good; the controller was released only on unmount. Fixed: failure is terminal and quiescent — the controller is detached and disposed (only if it actually opened: a controller whose initialize threw has no player, and the real dispose would wait forever on its unfinished creation). Test: a controller whose pause throws and stays playing ends in exactly one dispose.
  • The camera skeleton drew raw landmarks over the demonstration. FACT: _SkeletonOverlay read latestPoseFrameProvider, not the stabilised body the hand-over reads — with the toggle on, a partial room pose (the very "OUT OF CONTRACT" one from the S23) drew bone fragments over the clip: two figures on the panel. Fixed: the overlay is gated on the same coachBodyDrawableProvider. Tests: camera mode + toggle, partial frames → demo visible, no skeleton; a body → demo hidden, skeleton present.
