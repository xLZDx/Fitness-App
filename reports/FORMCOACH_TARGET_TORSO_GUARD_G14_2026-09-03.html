The device defect

A real squat set, S23, build 2965. Right after a completed rep, the lifter stepped back from the camera — and the target outline stretched into an unrecognisable shape, then froze on screen in that state (no new frames arriving, nothing to repaint over it).

Screenshots: reports/device-check-2026-09-02/s23_squat_result.png, s23_check2.png — identical two minutes apart, confirming the frame was genuinely frozen, not still changing.

alignTargetToBody already guarded the degenerate case (a zero-length torso — division blow-up) but had no upper bound at all. Each individual joint passed _drawable's own tolerance (±0.5 past the frame edge, sized for a genuinely extrapolated ankle) — but a pair of joints, each individually within tolerance, could still land near opposite corners of the widened box, which is exactly what happened.

The first diagnosis was wrong — and review is what caught it

The original fix rejected liveTorso > 1.2, justified by a [pose-probe] log line — a diagnostic that turned out to accumulate a bounding box across EVERY frame and EVERY landmark since the controller was built (never reset), not a measurement of any single frame. GPT-PM caught this in round 1: the cited numbers did not prove what they were cited to prove.

Rather than defend the diagnosis, a permanent per-frame [align] log was added directly in the placement function, and the defect was reproduced live on real hardware.

Why it took four review rounds

Every round caught something real — including one genuine reasoning error of my own, which GPT-PM corrected rather than simply rejected.

RoundVerdictFinding and response
1 MAJOR The 1.2 threshold was justified by an accumulator reading, not a single frame. A real defect was found instead: on a Mi 9T Pro (S23 unavailable), stepping out of frame produced real per-frame liveTorso 0.461-0.641 — 1.2 would not have rejected a single one of them. Threshold recomputed to 0.40 from real data.
2 MAJOR Only the reject side was proven. An absolute cutoff also has to not reject a genuinely valid closer body — and that was unproven. Two live attempts produced no clean sample in the 0.26-0.40 band: the one sustained cluster there (0.454-0.470) coincided with a real tracking failure (gate=lowConfidence).
3 MAJOR A fair correction of my own error: a rep with gate=ok and low match was lumped in with one that had gate=lowConfidence, as if both meant "unreliable." Those are different signals — a low match just means the pose fell short of the target, not that the coordinates are broken. Asked for one more attempt, aimed at a deliberately well-executed close squat.
4 APPROVE (final) A real frame (rep #25): gate=ok, match=0.210 (short of depth, not a tracking issue), liveTorso=0.332 — squarely inside the disputed band, correctly accepted by the guard. Gap closed with real measurements on both sides.
