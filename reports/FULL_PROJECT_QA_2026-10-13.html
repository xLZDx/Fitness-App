Four extra tasks

Operator taskOutcome
1. Exercise descriptions vs videos0 of 1887 exercises lack a description (summary and steps filled everywhere, the Russian overlay covers every id). The purpose field is empty for 1484. Video sample: 40 exercises, 36 match, 0 mismatch, 4 unclear. Problems: for 291 exercises the equipment named in the title disagrees with equipmentId (often bench_press on dumbbell/band moves), 83 ids in 38 groups share identical steps, 182 have no muscles, 3 truncated video names, 1 clip missing, 1 quarantined id still in the catalog file. Nothing needs descriptions added where 'missing' — it is purpose that is missing. Nothing added yet.
2. 100 accounts and honest selection100 deterministic profiles (not real accounts). Determinism: 0 differences. Distinct outputs: plan 32, suggestions 34, programme 70. Contraindication, equipment and level violations: 0. But: age is never read (20 minors and 10 seniors get the same output as a 30-year-old), 32 questionnaire fields change nothing, 89 yoga/stretch cards are labelled 'builds muscle/strength', 36 'filtered N for injury' texts also count equipment, a rib injury is silently ignored (6), 474 programme rows with bench_press go to home users without a bench (inference), 37 max-effort loads for rehab/mobility goals, 12 with no stretching and 10 with no cardio for such goals, 9 plans exceed the stated 45 minutes. Verdict: recommendations are safe on contraindications but NOT fully honest about age, reasons and goal.
3. Live coach: ≥10 exercises, one engineThe engine is already partly data-driven (poseTargetsByTag, MM-Fit measuredRepConfigs, repSignalsByTag); the squat is hard-coded in the default selection, depth signal, classifiers and FormCueKey. Data exists for squat, lunge, overhead press, bicep curl (push-up and crunch are below the 80% bar, the hip-hinge deadlift uses an authored signal). No data for bent-over row, lateral raise, plank, glute bridge, pull-up, step-up, dips; calf raise is excluded (ML Kit has no heel/toe). Needed: a JSON exercise registry, one generic classifier, ru/en message keys. Risks: 2D angles depend on camera view, no spine/rotation landmarks, medical wording. Not implemented.
4. Body-comparison screen from the videoThe video (2:36) shows a third-party reference app: distance/light/position checks → 3-2-1 countdown → auto shutter → three poses (front, side, back). The before/after view itself is empty in the clip. Fitness_App has encrypted storage, consent, review and ML Kit pose; it lacks auto-shutter, a three-pose sequence, scan history, keypoint alignment, slider/overlay. Plan: 8 steps, about 24–28 days (MVP 12–14), no new packages. The server-side body-composition estimate contradicts 'photos never leave the phone'. Not implemented.
