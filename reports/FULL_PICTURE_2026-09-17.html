⚠ The main new finding — today, Sep 17

You asked directly whether there is test-proven confidence that the live production equipment-recognition path (Gemini) is safe to show real users. A real measurement was run for the first time — and it did not match what had been assumed.

55.6%accuracy on high/moderate-confidence ground truth

The model twice misread legible on-machine text (a "SHOULDER PRESS" placard read as a lat pulldown at 0.85–0.88 confidence). Model confidence does not separate right from wrong: 0.950 average on correct answers vs. 0.877 on wrong ones — too small a gap to rely on.

Done today: the first fix attempt (a list of "unreliable" categories) turned out ineffective — it didn't touch any of the 4 actually measured failures. Following re-analysis and with your approval (decision FITAPP-EQUIP-ACC-2026-09-17), a stronger fix was adopted: every cloud (Gemini) match now always routes through "I'm not sure, you pick" (ScanOutcome.alternatives) instead of "confident," until a real end-to-end measurement of the production path exists. A confident result remains reachable only via the printed-text recognition path.

Not closed: the underlying classification-accuracy problem itself is unsolved — only hidden from the user behind an honest "not sure" framing. It needs either a larger dataset/calibration effort, or your explicit risk acceptance before wider distribution.
