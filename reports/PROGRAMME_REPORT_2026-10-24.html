1. The whole picture

  • The product: a Flutter gym app with a Firebase backend: equipment scan, personal recommendations, camera form coaching, nutrition, progress.
  • One programme in five eras: (1) Phase 1 MVP, May to October 2026 (62 sprint and gate rows); (2) Phase 2: branch split and sprints S00-S04; (3) the Body Scan and Nutrition sprint; (4) AI Coach and Gym Digital Twin (documents only so far); (5) the v1 FitnessUp restart since 2026-10-21 (56 tasks, slices V0 onward).
  • The vision list has 75 items. By code: exists 7, partial 26, flag off 5, not started 26, out of scope 10, not verified 1.
  • Phase 2 is delivered up to S04 only; S05-S28 are not started and not rescheduled, the project moved to the v1 line. The roadmap needs your re-baseline decision.
  • The app was never published to a store (Firebase App Distribution only). External and operator gates stand before the store: clinical validation, Stripe, store accounts, device.
  • Branches: 45 remote and 81 local. Not merged into master: 7 remote (6 are this week's work) and 41 local; some of that is valuable work that is not on master (section 6).
  • Documents disagree with each other: 14 contradictions between plans and state were found (section 7), e.g. the Body Scan state page and STATE.md lag behind git.
