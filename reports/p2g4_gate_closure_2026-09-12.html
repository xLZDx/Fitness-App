The two round-2 findings, in detail

Finding A — collapsed badge footprint
The badge's "small fixed size" requirement (OP-02 / task T6) was height-only; width was never bounded and the pill stretched edge-to-edge on WorkoutPlayerPage's list and, it turned out, on the scanner page too.
Root cause: HudSurface's own topHighlight branch wraps content in Stack(fit: StackFit.passthrough), and that Stack reports its size as the biggest its constraints allow, regardless of its child's actual size — confirmed empirically after two wrong hand-derivations (an Align without, then with, widthFactor — neither changed the measured width). Fixed with an explicit ConstrainedBox(maxWidth: 220, maxHeight: 60), applied only while collapsed, since T6's DoD text is specific to the collapsed state and constraining the expanded panel the same way risked clipping a longer detail line.
Finding B — progressive rendering order (task T2)
Round 1's fix for "don't gate identity on the generic ScanOutcome" also removed the !scan.isLoading boundary, letting the identity badge appear before the primary scan result had settled.
Restored by splitting the value into an unconditional resolvedIdentity and a displayed identity = scan.isLoading ? null : resolvedIdentity — keeps the independence fix from round 1 while restoring "UI renders type result before identity resolves" (T2). Proven with a Completer-backed fake service that holds the generic classification open on purpose.
