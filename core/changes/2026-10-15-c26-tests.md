## contrast_tokens_test.dart

* Defect fixed: `Color.toARGB32()` does not exist in the pinned Flutter SDK (compile error); replaced with `Color.value`.
* Added two source-pinned tests: the readiness panel fill alpha is parsed from `scan_capture_screen.dart` (must be >= 0.85 and the four status colours must still pass 4.5:1 over a white scene at that alpha), and `setup_status_panel.dart` may use only greenAccent, orangeAccent and white (the colours covered by the table). Without them the camera pairs used hard-coded constants and would stay green if the source regressed to 0.65.

Verification: `flutter test test/core/theme/contrast_tokens_test.dart --no-pub` from `mobile`.

## body_scan_plurals_test.dart

Renders each new plural key through the generated localizations for whole numbers (RU one/few/many for 1,2,5,11,21,22,25,100..112; EN one/other; zero cases), asserts decimals (0.4, 1.0, 1.5, 2.1, 5.0, 24.1) go through locale-formatted strings with a fixed non-inflecting unit (kg, п. п., нед.), and checks ARB structure (RU one/few/many/other, int placeholders, no double/num plural anywhere). `flutter test test/l10n/body_scan_plurals_test.dart --no-pub`.

## body_scan_a11y_test.dart

Pumps `ScanCaptureScreen` with the repo fakes (scan_fakes.dart) at 360x640, scale 2.0, RU and EN, feeds empty frames so all four readiness rows fail: no exception, shutter and Close on screen, >= 48 dp, outside `scan.panelScroll`, panel height <= half the screen, a real tap on the shutter starts the manual timer (cancel control appears, also >= 48 dp). A guard test asserts the panel really scrolls (maxScrollExtent > 0) so the overflow check is not vacuous, and the four rows are reachable. `SetupStatusPanel` alone at 1.5x/2.0x and at a 277 dp width (+30 percent text emulation). No product defect found.
