## Rules applied
- Units are non-inflecting (`{value} ккал`, `{value} г`), so decimals never need a plural form. The only plural (`foodCheckCount`) is integer-only and carries RU one/few/many/other.
- Wording is neutral: no "budget", "cheat" or "over/under budget" language; the secondary "room left" text is informational (D-78).
- Check-these chips have state words (needs checking / checked) for screen readers.
