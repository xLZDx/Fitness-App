# S02-06b change record: late sweep job and fence lifetime

Date: 2026-10-16. Story: S02-06b (design 6.1 steps 3, 5, 6; test ids F55, F58, F77, F80, F95).

## What changed

- New `functions/src/activity_streams/late_sweep.ts`:
  - `sweepLateActivityStreams` (every 5 minutes) walks `activity_account_state` documents whose `lateSweepAt` is due. The document is the durable job written at `deleteAccount` Step 1c, outside `users/{uid}`, so it survives `recursiveDelete` and a kill after Auth deletion.
  - Not-done job: sweeps `streams/<uid>/` and `quarantine/<uid>/` and ends with a FINAL recursive delete of `users/{uid}/{activities,activity_streams,activity_settings,activity_tombstones}`; all parts are attempted independently; any failure records bare `lastErrorCode` and `lateSweepAttempts`, leaves the job not done; `lateSweepDone` is set only after full success (earliest value kept).
  - Done job: fence removed at `removeFenceAfter = max(lateSweepDone + 1 h, authDeletedAt + 70 min)` by a compare-and-delete transaction. If `authDeletedAt` is missing the job derives it from Auth absence at its first run (run time); with the Auth user still present the fence is never removed and the run reports `authPresent`.
  - `recordAuthDeletedAt` (earliest value kept), `removeFenceAfterMs`, one structured log entry per run (counts, ages, bare codes; stuck = not done more than 1 h after `lateSweepAt`).
- `functions/src/index.ts`: Step 3b records `authDeletedAt` after Auth deletion (non-fatal); `sweepLateActivityStreams` exported.

## Known risk

Per design, the sweep runs 15 minutes after Step 1c even if the user aborted the deletion and still exists; their cloud activity data is then erased and the fence stays (stuck report). Reported to the lead.
