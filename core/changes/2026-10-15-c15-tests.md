## Product defect found and fixed (flow page, D-78)
`BodyScanFlowPage` wired "Delete scan" on the terminal failure card to `FlowEventKind.cancel`, which the pure machine rejects from `failed` (`isActive` is false), so Delete did nothing and the failed card stayed as a dead end (the scan was never deleted). Fix: `BodyScanFlowDriver.deleteFailedScan()` asks the job runner to cancel/delete the scan and resets the flow; the page calls it and closes. Tests: `body_scan_flow_page_test.dart` (Delete reaches the runner and leaves the card) and `body_scan_flow_driver_test.dart` (`deleteFailedScan`). The first run of the Delete test failed (`calls == []`) before the fix.
