# S02-07 change record: Dart GPX 1.1 encoder

Date: 2026-10-16. Story: S02-07 (design 7; test ids U17, U18, U20).

## What changed

- New `mobile/lib/features/activity/data/gpx_encoder.dart`: pure `GpxEncoder.encode(ActivitySession, {GpxAudience})` returning the sealed `GpxResult` (`GpxDocument(xml, pointCount)` or `GpxNoGeometry`). One `trk`/`trkseg`; per GPS sample a `trkpt` with `lat`/`lon` (8 decimals, fixed point), `ele` (nearest altitude sample within 5 s) and `time` (start plus offset, UTC).
- `GpxAudience.owner` writes the full series and, when heart rate exists locally, the Garmin TrackPointExtension `hr` (own data on own device, design 7). `GpxAudience.shared` trims `privacy.routeTrimMeters` from both ends along the route (haversine distance reused from `activity_merger.dart`) and never writes heart rate.
- No GPS stream, an empty one, or a shared export trimmed to nothing returns `GpxNoGeometry` (no file, no empty track; DQ-3).
- New `mobile/lib/features/activity/data/export_sampling.dart`: `nearestStreamValue` (binary search, tolerance 5 s), shared with the FIT encoder.
- Files live in `activity/data` as the design says (pure-layer guard: no dart:io, Flutter or sibling feature imports).

## Deviations

- Laps are not written to GPX (GPX 1.1 has no lap element); they are in FIT.

## Verification state

No tests, builds or checks were run (operator rule v4).
