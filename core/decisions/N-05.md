# Decision: `N-05`

**Decision: 3a + 4, App Check enforcement deferred (not now).**

## 3a — budget alert

**Already satisfied.** The operator's own Google Cloud billing console
(`console.cloud.google.com/billing/.../budgets?project=fitness-app-korostelev`, screenshot
reviewed live 2026-09-17) shows a `"Firebase Project fitness-app-korostelev"` budget of $20/month,
triggering alerts at 50/90/100%, with `Spend cap status: Not applicable` — confirming this is a
notification-only alert, not an automatic service-pausing cap (Google's own UI text: *"Set your
budget with a spend cap to pause services if the caps are exceeded"* — not configured here). This
is exactly what `core/review/N05_DISPOSITION.md`'s Option 3a asks for. No further action needed.

## 4 — accept the residual risk

**Accepted.** Full-library extraction of the video corpus costs an estimated $0.077–$0.31
(`core/review/N05_DISPOSITION.md` §3), and at the project's current scale (5–10 testers, no real
users yet) this is judged not worth building additional abuse machinery for. The video licence's
own condition — no raw files, public folders, or permanent downloadable links — is unaffected by
this decision: the app's signed-URL mechanism (`functions/src/video_urls.ts`, 15-minute TTL, no
permanent links) already satisfies it, and the operator separately confirmed the video assets
themselves are officially licensed/purchased.

## App Check enforcement (`core/review/N05_DISPOSITION.md` Option 1 / N05-C) — deferred, not authorized now

**Not authorized in this decision.** `core/review/OPERATOR_DECISIONS.md`'s own N05-C guidance
("not yet, and it is a sequence rather than a date... the real precondition is stricter than 'at
Play launch'") is narrower than it needs to be as written today: the 2026-08-29 S23 Play Integrity
proof (`core/DECISION_LOG.md`, "S23 Play Integrity acceptance test: PASSED") showed the project's
App Check console is configured for `MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY` (not `PLAY_RECOGNIZED`/`LICENSED`),
so a release-signed APK installed outside Google Play on an ordinary, unmodified device (locked
bootloader) attests successfully — the risk of enabling today is narrower than "locks out the
entire current population," and is closer to "locks out non-standard devices (unlocked
bootloader/rooted, like the S8 dev device)."

That correction does not by itself make enabling urgent. Operator instruction, 2026-09-17, in
response to being asked for a production redeploy GO: *"а зачем это щас делать, там еще полно
багов и много чего еще доделывать или переделывать куда торопиться?"* — there is no reason to push
a live production config change into a project that still has open engineering work elsewhere.
**App Check enforcement for video stays off for now**, revisited whenever App Check is actually
being pursued for another reason, or before public beta / materially broader distribution — the
same pre-existing standing trigger `core/review/N05_DISPOSITION.md` already names.

## Who decided, and how

Recorded from a live, in-session exchange with the operator (the product's sole owner —
`korostelevivan@gmail.com`), 2026-09-17. The operator asked plain-language clarifying questions
about what the video is, why cost could accrue, and what the $0.077–$0.31 figure actually measures
(confirmed: worst case for the entire library, not a per-tester cost), reviewed their own GCP
billing console live, and confirmed the 3a+4 closure while explicitly declining to authorize the
App Check production redeploy today.

## A related, out-of-scope idea recorded for the backlog, not decided here

The operator raised moving video hosting off Google Cloud Storage to a self-managed VPS
(`"мне дешевле платить 25 баксов за вм и не думать о лимитах"`) as a target production
architecture. This does not resolve the video licence condition on its own — the same private,
time-limited-link requirement (`video_urls.ts:9-12`) applies regardless of where the bytes are
hosted — and moving to a single VPS without a CDN in front of it would trade the current
egress-cost exposure for a concurrency/bandwidth-saturation risk instead (a burst of simultaneous
viewers on one video would compete with the rest of the app for the VM's fixed network interface).
Recorded as a backlog item (target architecture: VPS + CDN in front of video), not evaluated or
decided further here — see the roadmap/backlog entry this decision points to.

## What this closes and what it does not

This closes the `N-05` row in `core/CURRENT_STATE.md` as an operator decision on the
budget-alert/residual-risk question (3a+4). It does **not** close or authorize the App Check
production configuration change — that stays a separate, not-yet-authorized action, tracked as its
own future decision rather than as part of this one.
