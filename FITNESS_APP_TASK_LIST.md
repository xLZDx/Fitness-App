# Fitness App — Complete Task List

**Concept:** Create QR codes for gym equipment from various manufacturers in sports halls to help people train correctly. The app pairs equipment with celebrity-led instructional videos, personalized recommendations, and a full ecosystem for training, nutrition, community, and rewards.

---

## Core Product

1. Create QR codes for gym equipment from various manufacturers in sports halls to help people train correctly.

2. Film videos with various celebrities from the sports world and beyond, who will demonstrate how to perform exercises correctly on the equipment.

3. Upon registration, create a comprehensive set of initial filters to tailor the experience to each individual based on their age, health status, physical strength and endurance, injuries, and so on. Suggested Questionnaire List:

   **Personal Information:**
   - 3.1. Age
   - 3.2. Gender
   - 3.3. Height
   - 3.4. Weight (current, target)
   - 3.5. Activity Level (sedentary, moderately active, active, very active)

   **Health & Medical History:**
   - 3.6. Pre-existing medical conditions (e.g., heart conditions, diabetes, asthma)
   - 3.7. Allergies (food, medication)
   - 3.8. Current medications
   - 3.9. Past or current injuries (specify body part and type, e.g., knee injury, back pain)
   - 3.10. Physical limitations or restrictions (e.g., cannot lift heavy weights, avoid certain movements)
   - 3.11. Any recent surgeries or medical procedures
   - 3.12. Blood pressure (high/low)
   - 3.13. Any other health concerns

   **Fitness Goals:**
   - 3.14. Weight loss
   - 3.15. Muscle gain
   - 3.16. Endurance improvement
   - 3.17. Strength building
   - 3.18. Flexibility/Mobility
   - 3.19. General fitness/Well-being
   - 3.20. Specific sport performance

   **Current Fitness Level:**
   - 3.21. How often do you exercise per week?
   - 3.22. What types of exercise do you currently do?
   - 3.23. How would you describe your current fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)?
   - 3.24. Can you perform basic exercises (e.g., push-ups, squats, planks)?

   **Lifestyle & Habits:**
   - 3.25. Dietary preferences/restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free)
   - 3.26. Smoking/Alcohol consumption
   - 3.27. Sleep patterns (hours per night)
   - 3.28. Stress levels
   - 3.29. Occupation (sedentary/active)

   **Equipment Access:**
   - 3.30. Do you have access to a gym?
   - 3.31. Do you have home exercise equipment? (If yes, specify)

   **Motivation & Preferences:**
   - 3.32. What motivates you to exercise?
   - 3.33. What type of workout environment do you prefer (e.g., high-intensity, relaxed, group classes)?
   - 3.34. Preferred workout duration

4. The application will be subscription-based.

5. Develop a very lightweight and modern interface to ensure the most comfortable user experience.

6. **Use Case:** A person enters a gym and encounters unfamiliar equipment. The equipment has a QR code. The person scans it, and a menu appears with suggestions and recommendations based on their profile (age, weight, injuries, illnesses, limitations, etc.). The user selects recommended workouts and enters the workout section. Different subscription tiers will allow choosing who to train with (a celebrity/Olympic champion or a regular trainer).

7. Create a network and training materials for sponsors, and display exercises when scanning codes at public sports grounds and sponsor venues, etc.

8. The database should contain thousands of videos for various gym equipment and exercises.

9. For the pilot phase, build a scraper to go through video hosting platforms and collect a library of free workouts for various gym equipment. Create a database for classifying equipment and workouts (the same piece of equipment might be from different manufacturers and have slight configuration differences).

10. Add a gym calendar with workout and diet reminders.

11. Build diet and workout plans as a comprehensive module with recommendations based on the questionnaire and tests (all possible health, weight, load, allergy, medication, lifestyle, etc., tests should be added as filters or input parameters for optimal comprehensive recommendations).

12. Add a "deep fake" option – with this option, clients can choose a deep fake of their favorite athlete, actor, politician, comedian, etc., to conduct workouts. This means the workout videos will feature the chosen character (all must be legally compliant).

13. Implement a free trial and various subscription plans depending on the product features.

14. Develop a loyalty system and rewards for loyalty.

15. Organize partnership discount programs for app users; for example, a discount at a fitness club through an in-app promotion (10% of the contract goes to the app).

16. Create a system to reward achievements; introduce ranks for accomplishments (e.g., 100 push-ups, ran 20 km, swam 10 km, lifted 100 kg, etc.).

17. Develop a billing and subscription system with flexible pricing plans and the ability to pay by any method (cash/card/cryptocurrencies).

18. Create an advertising module for partners: sports goods stores and gyms.

19. Create a mini-social network for people to communicate based on interests, equipment, exercises, etc. This can also be used for VIP training sessions with a live trainer.

20. Build an exercise performance monitoring system, where a camera tracks the user's movements, and the app provides voice and visual corrections for errors.

21. Make the application compatible with all modern health-tracking devices, such as heart rate monitors, step counters, etc., and build recommendations based on their data.

22. Add challenges for individuals and groups on gym equipment for bonuses, catering to those who may or may not know what to do.

23. Create a dynamic database of all sports venues, events, and competitions based on location, so that if a person travels to another city or country, they can automatically find all nearby sports events, register, buy tickets, and so on.

24. Implement a support program for people with disabilities.

25. Implement group programs for companies.

26. Track and accumulate all data from fitness trackers, gym tests, and manual input to monitor progress. It should show a history from the initial registration, indicating where the user was a year ago across various metrics and where they are now, with a graph/trend.

27. Consider and plan workouts at home without equipment; create a "Workout at Home" section and gather a vast collection of exercises that can be done at home without spending a penny on sports equipment, achieving great results.

28. Integrate a healthy eating/supplements store and delivery service, adding subscriptions for supplements based on workout plans, intensity, nutrition, and health status.

29. Create marketing channels on all popular platforms to promote the app and a healthy lifestyle, including at least one new post with a new exercise daily, and special features with celebrities.

30. Start developing merchandise for gyms and outdoor activities.

31. Implement a reward system to attract new users or clients to gyms.

32. Implement rewards for those who film professional workout videos and share them with others in the app (a partnership contract for advertising revenue and profits can be added).

33. The application should support at least 20 of the most common languages. All content and videos should adapt to the selected language, and users from different countries should see sports events based on their country of residence as a priority, with notifications for subscribed events.

34. Add the ability to buy tickets for any sports event worldwide (partnership agreements with organizers will be needed).

## Safety & Medical

35. **Pre-workout medical clearance flow** — for users with flagged conditions (heart issues, recent surgery, pregnancy), require a doctor's sign-off upload or telehealth consult before unlocking high-intensity plans.

36. **Emergency SOS button** — one-tap call to emergency services with auto-shared GPS location and medical profile (allergies, conditions, blood type, emergency contact).

37. **Injury recovery / rehab mode** — physiotherapist-designed protocols for common injuries (ACL, rotator cuff, lower back) with progress gates before returning to full load.

38. **Real-time form-risk detection** — extend the camera monitoring (task 20) to halt the session and warn when it detects dangerous form (e.g., spinal flexion under load).

39. **Overtraining / recovery score** — combine HRV, sleep, soreness self-report, and load to recommend rest days; prevents burnout and injury.

---

## AI & Personalization

40. **AI workout generator** — daily plan auto-adapts to last session's RPE, missed days, sleep, and cycle phase (for women).

41. **AI chatbot coach** — 24/7 Q&A for "is this pain normal?", "can I swap squats for X?", form tips between sessions.

42. **Menstrual cycle sync** — adjust intensity, nutrition, and recovery recommendations across cycle phases.

43. **Voice-guided workouts** — hands-free audio coaching for runs, cycling, and equipment use where looking at a screen is inconvenient.

---

## Wearables & Biometrics

44. **Apple Health / Google Fit / Garmin / Whoop / Oura deep integration** — explicitly named in task 21 but worth calling out as separate certified integrations, each with their own approval workflow.

45. **Smart scale & body composition tracking** — sync with Withings, Renpho, etc., for body fat %, muscle mass trends.

46. **Continuous glucose monitor (CGM) integration** — for diabetic users and metabolic-health enthusiasts; tie meal/workout timing to glucose response.

---

## Content & Community

47. **Live-streamed group classes** — scheduled real-time sessions with trainers, leaderboards, and chat (Peloton-style).

48. **User-generated workout marketplace** — certified trainers sell their programs; revenue share with the platform.

49. **Trainer certification & verification system** — vet credentials before trainers can sell content or run live sessions.

50. **Workout buddy matching** — pair users by goals, schedule, level, and gym location for accountability.

51. **Family / household plans** — shared subscription with separate profiles for kids, teens, seniors with age-appropriate content.

52. **Kids & teens mode** — age-gated content, parental controls, school-PE-aligned programs.

53. **Senior / 60+ mode** — joint-safe, balance-focused, fall-prevention programs.

54. **Prenatal & postnatal programs** — trimester-specific routines reviewed by OB-GYNs.

## Gamification & Engagement

55. **Streak tracking & habit formation mechanics** — daily/weekly streaks, freeze tokens, comeback bonuses.

56. **Virtual coins / in-app currency** — earn through workouts, spend on merch (task 30), supplements (task 28), or premium content unlocks.

57. **Seasonal events & limited-time challenges** — "summer shred", "January reboot" with exclusive rewards.

58. **Avatar / character customization** — visual representation that levels up with the user's real-world progress.

## Business & Monetization

59. **Corporate wellness B2B portal** — companies (task 25) need an admin dashboard, anonymized aggregate analytics, and HRIS integration.

60. **Insurance partnerships** — discounted premiums for users hitting activity targets (data-sharing with explicit consent).

61. **Gym/equipment manufacturer SaaS tier** — manufacturers pay to have official content tied to their QR codes; analytics on which equipment is used most.

62. **Affiliate program for influencers** — beyond celebrity videos (task 2), micro-influencers earn per signup.

63. **Referral-to-earn** — existing users earn free months for bringing friends.

---

## Compliance, Trust & Operations

64. **GDPR / HIPAA / regional health-data compliance framework** — health data is heavily regulated; this is a foundational requirement, not a feature.

65. **Age verification & parental consent (COPPA)** — required if minors use the app.

66. **Content moderation system** — for the social network (task 19) and user-uploaded videos (task 32): NSFW filtering, harassment reports, takedown workflow.

67. **Deep fake consent & legal framework** — for task 12: licensing agreements, watermarking, anti-misuse safeguards (mandatory disclosure that content is synthetic).

68. **Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)** — screen-reader support, captions on every video, color-blind palettes, voice control. Adjacent to task 24 but applies to the whole app.

69. **Offline mode** — download workouts for use in gyms with poor signal or while traveling.

---

## Hardware & Physical Layer

70. **NFC tags as alternative to QR** — faster scanning, no camera framing needed; useful for outdoor/wet environments.

71. **AR overlay for equipment** — point camera at machine, see live overlay showing seat height, grip position, and range of motion.

72. **Smart-mirror / TV cast support** — Chromecast, AirPlay, Samsung Health TV apps.

---

## Data & Analytics

73. **Personal data export & deletion** — required by GDPR; users own their training history.

74. **Annual / quarterly progress reports** — auto-generated PDFs (Spotify Wrapped style) — high shareability, drives organic growth.

75. **Coach-shareable view** — give a personal trainer or doctor a read-only link to your data.
