## 1. Decisions taken for the fix pass (operator may override; all are defaults)

| # | Decision | Reason |

### Decisions Y1..Y11 (defaults; the operator may override)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| Y1 | Keep on a revoked recording = seal and hand off as a finished partial activity (the data up to the revocation is readable). Quarantine (file untouched, clears the strip and the Start block) applies only to `recoveryFailed`. R-024, S04-19 AC-2, contract and design all say so. Delete removes the recording. |
| Y2 | Hand-off before unbind has a mechanism: the bind controller in `activity_wiring.dart` awaits a registered recorder pre-unbind hook (bounded, PROPOSED 5 s) before it unbinds on sign-out, uid change or deletion; the hook seals and hands off with the owner check. If the hook times out or fails, the unsealed recording stays in its scope with the 30-day expiry. The sign-out guard path uses the same hook. |
| Y3 | A transient auth null or loading state keeps the recorder running; appends refused while the scope is unbound go to a bounded in-memory buffer (PROPOSED 120 s) and flush after the same uid rebinds; past the bound the recording stops with the reason `account`. The recording journal keeps its own durable wiped-uid marker (it does not rely on the in-memory `_wipedUid`). |
