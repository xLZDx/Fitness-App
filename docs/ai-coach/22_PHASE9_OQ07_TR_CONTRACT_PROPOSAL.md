## Non-goals

- Do not choose load, repetition, rest, age, progression, or symptom thresholds.
- Do not reject or rewrite a structurally valid fact that the user actually performed merely because it is outside a prescription ceiling; classify and route it without falsifying the fact.
- Do not treat an existing heuristic, age policy, progression helper, or model output as signed safety policy.
- Do not enable symptom-stop behavior until the symptom scope and actual numeric values have an authorized qualified-human signature.

| Option | Behavior | Assessment |

## Proposed technical boundary

1. **Policy envelope.** A policy is usable only through an explicit, versioned record containing a stable policy ID and version, content digest, scope, approval-record reference, qualified-signer reference, approval/effective timestamps, expiry or supersession state, and the policy-schema version. This proposal defines no numeric limit, symptom value, or credential standard. A missing/unverifiable approval reference, signer qualification, scope match, version, or active state makes the policy unavailable; an unsigned heuristic is never promoted to policy.
2. **Freshness and invalidation.** Before assignment, resolve the policy against the current user/context identity and its authoritative record. Reject a policy that is not yet effective, is expired, revoked or superseded, has a mismatched scope/version, or cannot be checked against its authoritative source. The maximum age for mutable supporting inputs must be an explicit versioned policy field; if that value or a required source timestamp is absent, the input is not current and the assignment fails closed. No hidden client default or elapsed-time guess may supply freshness.
3. **Assignment gate.** Every value that can become an actionable recommendation must pass one policy gate before it is exposed as an assignment. The sourced paths are: `generatedPlanProvider` -> `buildPlan` -> `PlanReady`; `SafetyGate.check` inside `dispatch` step 4 for `start_workout`, `start_set` targets and `replace_exercise`; progression suggestions from `suggestNextWeight`; model-proposed loads; any future multi-week composer; and any plan loaded by `start_workout`. The gate validates load/repetition/rest against the applicable approved policy and emits a typed refusal/unavailable result when policy or required inputs are absent, invalid, stale, contradictory, or out of policy. Existing `evaluateExercise`, age-band policy and progression values are eligibility/intensity heuristics, not signed maxima; the current gate does not prove an approved OQ-07 policy is enforced. The current closed command set has no separate load-assignment command; `record_set` and `correct_set` are performed-fact operations and must not be converted into prescription checks. This path map describes the required future enforcement boundary and does not claim the current interfaces enforce it.
4. **Structural validity versus policy outcome.** The performed-set structural schema is separate from prescription policy: canonical non-empty exercise ID; integer repetitions `0..1000` (the existing design bound); finite IEEE-754 binary64 `weight_kg >= 0`, or `null` only for a bodyweight set; closed source enum `manual|voice|form_coach|wearable`; and a valid command ID/provenance envelope. The finite representation is the structural bound for weight; no policy/clinical ceiling is applied to a performed fact. A malformed event returns `INVALID_PERFORMED_FACT` and stores nothing. A structurally valid performed fact is recorded unchanged even when the applicable assignment policy cannot be verified; its classification is `POLICY_CLASSIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`, never `WITHIN_POLICY`, `RECORDED_OUT_OF_POLICY`, or clinically safe. The journal atomically associates the fact and provenance with its classification status, or records an explicit pending/unavailable classification state if atomic association is not supported. After a policy is verified, deterministic reclassification uses that versioned policy and remains independently auditable; it never silently changes the performed fact. Only when a verified applicable policy classifies the fact outside its assignment ceiling may the result be `RECORDED_OUT_OF_POLICY` with a separate `SAFETY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` marker. These names are proposed result codes, not claims about current implementation. They never imply that the fact or activity was safe, do not silently clamp/rewrite the report, and do not block `record_set`, `correct_set` or `void_set` solely because of an assignment ceiling.
5. **Requirements reconciliation.** AICR-029 now separates structural rejection, proposed-assignment policy refusal, and recording an out-of-policy performed fact. AICR-082's performed-fact allowance remains in force. The authorized `functional-test-reviewer` has revised C7 and its linked FT-1023/1024/1053 rows to describe those three acceptance cases separately; FT-1024 asserts preservation/classification of the fact, not an assignment-cap refusal. This remains planned coverage only; the result-code names remain open and no test execution is claimed. `FT-1053` remains blocked for implementation-level assignment behavior until the actual assignment path and signed policy exist; test authors own further coverage changes through the authorized `functional-test-reviewer`/`e2e-runner` route.
6. **Symptoms and explanations.** Symptom handling uses only a separately defined and signed scope. If policy, scope, or qualified signature is unavailable, no symptom threshold or list is inferred, and no clinical-safety claim is made. Any user-visible statement that a value is safe, clinically appropriate, or approved must be a deterministic rendering of a traceable result from the applicable signed policy; model prose cannot create that conclusion.

## Failure modes and recovery

| Failure | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| No approved policy/version for a proposed assignment | Typed refusal/unavailable; create no actionable assignment |
| Policy or required context is stale, contradictory, or invalid | Typed fail-closed result; request clarification or policy correction |
| Proposed assignment exceeds a current signed policy | `REFUSED_POLICY`; create no actionable assignment |
| Performed fact is structurally invalid (for example, NaN or invalid representation) | `INVALID_PERFORMED_FACT`; store nothing |
| Performed fact is structurally valid but signed-policy classification is unavailable | Store the fact unchanged with provenance and `POLICY_CLASSIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE` atomically, or an explicit pending/unavailable classification state; never claim it is within/outside policy or clinically safe |
| A previously unavailable performed fact can now be classified under a verified versioned policy | Reclassify deterministically and audit the classification/version separately; never mutate the fact |
| Performed fact is structurally valid and a verified policy classifies it outside an assignment ceiling | Preserve the exact fact; return `RECORDED_OUT_OF_POLICY` and the policy-defined review marker; never clamp it or claim safety |
| Symptom scope or qualified signer is missing | Keep symptom limits unavailable; do not infer values or claim approval |
| Policy result cannot be traced to its version/source | Refuse the claim-bearing result and retain no unsupported safety assertion |

### Codex technical disposition — policy-resolution interface

The operator delegated the task-local technical contract to Codex. The following interface decisions close the technical boundary without selecting clinical values, naming a signer, or claiming that a current code path implements it:

1. A single `PrescriptionPolicyResolver` boundary resolves a policy for the authenticated user, assignment purpose, target plan version and applicable context version. The resolver reads only the authoritative policy source configured for that environment; model output, client-provided policy objects, eligibility heuristics and cached unsigned values are never authoritative. The concrete source adapter remains absent, so the resolver currently returns `POLICY_UNAVAILABLE` for every actionable assignment.
2. A verified policy result is an immutable envelope containing `schemaVersion`, `policyId`, `policyVersion`, `contentDigest`, exact subject/purpose scope, `approvalRecordRef`, `qualifiedSignerRef`, `approvedAt`, `effectiveAt`, optional `expiresAt`, and revocation/supersession status. Qualification and approval references must resolve through the trusted authorization registry before use. The contract does not prescribe a profession, credential, numerical limit, symptom list or clinical meaning; those require the qualified human's signed policy.
3. Resolution returns one closed result: `VERIFIED_ACTIVE(policyEnvelope)`, `NOT_FOUND`, `UNVERIFIABLE_SOURCE`, `INVALID_SIGNATURE_OR_APPROVAL`, `UNQUALIFIED_SIGNER`, `SCOPE_MISMATCH`, `NOT_YET_EFFECTIVE`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `UNSUPPORTED_SCHEMA`. Only `VERIFIED_ACTIVE` proceeds to assignment validation. Every other result refuses the actionable assignment and exposes no proposed dose as an authorized prescription.
4. Existing `evaluateExercise`, age eligibility and progression checks remain necessary preconditions, not OQ-07 approval. An existing block remains a block; an allow result merely permits the next signed-policy check. An unknown/incomplete precondition returns the existing typed unavailable/refusal and never falls through to a heuristic cap. A saved plan is revalidated against the policy identity/version before activation.
5. Performed-set writes keep their separate structural-validation path. A valid report of an already performed set is preserved unchanged even when no assignment policy exists or a signed assignment ceiling would be exceeded. When signed-policy classification is unavailable, record `POLICY_CLASSIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE` (never `WITHIN_POLICY`, `RECORDED_OUT_OF_POLICY`, or clinically safe) and atomically retain fact, provenance, and classification, or an explicit pending/unavailable classification state. Reclassification after policy verification is deterministic and independently auditable, without mutating the fact. Invalid structure is rejected without write.
